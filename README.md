# Momentum 🚀

A study tracker made by a 17 year old student who got tired of pretending productivity apps actually understand students.

## What is this?

Momentum is a study tracking app I built because I was frustrated.

Most productivity apps felt like:

> "Wake up at 5AM 😎 become sigma"

Bro I just wanted something that helps me survive JEE/MHT-CET life 😭

So I made this.

The idea is simple:

**small study sessions + consistency + momentum > motivation**

Because motivation disappears after 2 days.

Momentum tries to help students stay consistent and understand how they actually study.

---

## Features

- 📚 Study session tracking
- 🔥 Momentum / streak system
- 😵 Mood tracking (productive / distracted / tired etc.)
- 📊 Analytics
- ⭐ XP system because brain likes dopamine
- 📁 Export study data as CSV
- 🧠 Subject wise tracking
- 🌙 Dark mode because most students study at 2AM

---

## Why did I make this?

I am a student myself.

Preparing for competitive exams made me realize something:

Most students don't fail because they are dumb.

They fail because:
- inconsistency
- burnout
- no system
- random study patterns
- motivation dependency

So I thought:

> "Why not make something for students by a student?"

And here we are.

---

## Philosophy of this app

This app is based on one simple belief:

> Momentum matters more than motivation.

Even 30 minutes done consistently beats fake 12-hour productivity videos.

Small progress counts.

Bad days happen.

But if momentum survives, you're still moving.
